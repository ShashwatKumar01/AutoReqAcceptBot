## 🌟 Why Auto Request Approver?

Telegram Channels and Supergroups using **Join Request links** frequently accumulate thousands of pending requests. If users aren't approved quickly, they lose interest, forget they requested to join, or migrate to competitor channels.

**Auto Request Approver Bot** (`@RequestApproverAuto_bot`) automates the entire onboarding pipeline:
1. **Instant or Delayed Auto-Approval**: Approves join requests 24/7 without manual intervention.
2. **Private Welcome DM**: Sends a personalized message directly to the new subscriber's DM upon approval.
3. **Audience Retargeting**: Builds a persistent database of all approved members, allowing you to broadcast future announcements and promotional offers directly to their DMs!

## 🚀 Key Features

### ⚡ 1. Zero-Latency Auto-Approval
- Seamlessly handles thousands of concurrent join requests across multiple channels and groups.
- **Instant Mode**: Approves join requests in less than a second.
- **Configurable Delay**: Optionally delay approvals (e.g. 10s, 1m, 10m) to simulate manual review or maximize marketing pacing.

### 💌 2. Personalized Private Welcome DM
- Automatically sends a warm greeting to the user's private chat as soon as their join request is approved.
- **Dynamic Variables**: Personalize messages with `{name}`, `{username}`, `{chat_title}`, and `{mention}`.
- **Rich Media & Formatting**: Supports photos, GIFs, videos, bold text, italics, and custom Telegram emojis.

### 🔘 3. Interactive Inline Buttons
- Attach custom URL buttons to your welcome message directing users to:
  - Your other Telegram channels/groups.
  - Special promotional offers & affiliate deals.
  - Websites, apps, or social media pages.

### 📢 4. Audience-Segmented Mass Broadcasts
- Reach your audience directly in their private chats!
- **Target Segments**:
  - `🌐 All Users`: Every user who ever requested to join or interacted with the bot.
  - `📁 Channel-Specific`: Target members who joined a specific channel.
  - `👑 Chat Admins`: Send admin-only notifications.
  - `🎯 Specific ID`: Send a targeted message to a single user.
- **Anti-Flood Safe**: 25 messages/second pacing to strictly comply with Telegram rate limits.

### 📊 5. Real-Time Tracking & Analytics
- Live statistics of total approved requests, daily growth, and active chats.
- Track conversions across various private invite links.

### 👑 6. Multi-Channel & Multi-Admin Support
- Manage unlimited channels and supergroups from a single control interface.
- Add co-admins and moderators with custom permission levels.

## 🛠️ Quick Start Guide (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Add Bot as Admin
1. Open your Telegram Channel or Supergroup.
2. Go to **Channel Settings** ➔ **Administrators** ➔ **Add Administrator**.
3. Search for [@RequestApproverAuto_bot](https://t.me/RequestApproverAuto_bot) and add it.
4. Grant the bot permission to **"Add Users"** / **"Manage Join Requests"**.

### Step 2: Enable "Approve New Members"
1. In your Channel Settings, go to **Invite Links**.
2. Create a new link and toggle **"Request Admin Approval"** to ON.
3. Share this link on ads, social media, or other channels.

### Step 3: Configure Welcome Message
1. Open [@RequestApproverAuto_bot](https://t.me/RequestApproverAuto_bot) in Telegram.
2. Send `/start` and select your channel.
3. Set your custom welcome message and inline buttons.
4. **Done!** Every new join request is now approved and welcomed automatically!
